WELCOME TO YOUR ATELIER

Find your rhythm.

1. Choose your words. Choose a French, Italian, English, or Spanish passage, or import your own text. Interface and passage languages are independent.

2. Click the passage and type. Completed characters turn teal. Mistakes turn red, with the character you typed shown above. Backspace lets you correct them.

3. Follow the outlined key. Matching fingers share a color on both hands. Large keys have no finger band. K in BÉPO and B in QWERTY are guided on the left side only.

My keyboard reads the characters your keyboard actually sends. Select the visual layout that matches your system or keyboard firmware.

Simulate layout maps physical key positions to the selected BÉPO or US QWERTY layout inside this app. It does not change your operating system. In BÉPO, hold AltGr for alternate symbols. Type ê with the circumflex key followed by e.

Dvorak (US) works with every keyboard shape. Choose My keyboard with system Dvorak, or Simulate layout to practise using physical key positions.

Two themes. Click the nameplate to switch between France and Italia. Your passage and progress stay in place.

The US QWERTY simulation uses standard US symbols. For French, Italian, or Spanish accents, use your system input method in native mode, or practise with BÉPO simulation. Characters without a key guide are labelled explicitly.

Fn is shown as a keyboard reference; its hardware functions stay on your keyboard.

Layout references: BÉPO key map and BÉPO manual. Keyboard shape and palette adapted from your photographs.

Advanced search

Search matches passage titles only, ignoring case. Words can match part of a title.

morning AND Italy
Both words must appear. A space between words also means AND.
Italy OR Spain
Either word can appear.
evening NOT blue
Include the first word and exclude the second.
"blue evening"
Match the words together in this order.
(Italy OR Spain) AND morning
Group alternatives, then require another word.

NOT is applied before AND, then OR. Parentheses override this order. Operators also work in lowercase. Quote a word like "AND" to search for it literally.
